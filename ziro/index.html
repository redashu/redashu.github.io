Ziro needs a permit — Meghalaya doesn't

Ziro Valley sits inside Arunachal Pradesh, so every Indian traveller who isn't a resident needs an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to enter — there's no route around a check gate. Apply online at arunachalilp.com (about ₹100, ready in 1–2 days, valid 15–30 days). Meghalaya, where the rest of this trip happens, needs no permit at all.

Wei Sawdong: reopened, watch it anyway

Wei Sawdong Falls near Sohra has a history of temporary closures from a land dispute between neighbouring villages — most recently a closure in early July 2026 that was lifted on July 14. It should be open on Sep 30, but this has flipped before, so the Day 2 plan below has a ready swap.

Cash before the river

Down at Shnongpdeng and along the Dawki border stretch, mobile signal and card machines are patchy to non-existent. Carry enough cash in a waterproof pouch before you reach the riverside camps.
